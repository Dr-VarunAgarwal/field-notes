Glasgow & Edinburgh

26–27 October, then 1 November 2025

A camera-only walk, no phone snapshots — a first pass through Glasgow's Necropolis and Kelvingrove, then a return trip a few days later for one long circuit round Calton Hill, looking down over all of Edinburgh at once.

I

26–27 October

Kelvingrove, then the Necropolis

Kelvingrove Park

Glasgow Necropolis

The graveyard sits right on a hill over the city — its own tree-lined avenues, then a clear drop down to the rooftops and steam below.

II

1 November

One long stop on Calton Hill

Looking west, over the Old Town

The Balmoral's clock tower, the Castle on its ridge, the Scott Monument's spire and the curved silver roofline of the W Edinburgh, all in one frame.

The National Monument

Turning north, then south

The same hilltop turns a full circle — the Firth of Forth and Leith's docks one way, Arthur's Seat and the Parliament's tented roof the other.

Holyrood

Down into town

Off the hill and back into the crowds on Princes Street, the Scott Monument closing the walk the way the National Monument opened it.

Two cities,
one roll apart.
