Isle of Skye

7–10 June 2018

The train up from Inverness, then four days working anticlockwise round Skye — a castle on the mainland side, the Cuillins from Sligachan, waterfalls and cliffs on the west coast, then the Trotternish ridge, before heading back over the water and inland past Glenfinnan.

V

10 June, later

Back over the water, toward Glenfinnan

Skye behind, the road and rail line home cut through Lochaber — past the Glenfinnan Viaduct, the "Harry Potter train" crossing it right on cue, and a stop at the roadside grave known locally as "Dumbledore's grave."

Slower than planned.
Better for it.

Honourable mention: the music discovered along the way — Martyn Bennett's Blackbird and Liberation, both a real find on this trip.
