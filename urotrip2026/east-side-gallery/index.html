Wander · eUrope · on the wall

East Side Gallery, one wall.

The murals painted on the longest surviving stretch of the Berlin Wall, in the order you'd actually pass them walking it — not curated into a gallery grid. A few things that aren't murals at all sit in between: a plaque, a sign, a bridge, a sculpture in the river — the glances away from the wall that are part of actually being there.

18 of ~1300m, Sept 2. A first pass through the roll — the wall itself runs for over a kilometer and this covers a fraction of it. More panels to come from the same shoot.
