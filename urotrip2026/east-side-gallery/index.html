Wander · eUrope · on the wall

My walk along Berlin's artistic wall.

Street art, as political as it gets — the longest surviving stretch of the Berlin Wall, painted over almost end to end. Most of these murals date to right after the Wall came down in 1990, later restored as the paint faded. In the order I actually passed them walking it, not curated into a gallery grid. A few things that aren't murals at all sit in between: a plaque, a sign, a bridge, a sculpture in the river — the glances away from the wall that are part of actually being there. Panel #28 is the one everyone already knows, even if not by name: the Fraternal Kiss.
