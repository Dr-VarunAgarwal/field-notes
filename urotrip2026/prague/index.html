The Clock.

Three days in and around Prague, set on one clock face at the minute each photo was taken — the train in along the Elbe, a race night in the Old Town, an afternoon at the castle that ran on down to Letná. Stacked together they make one long afternoon that turns into night.

Drag the gold hand round the dial, tap any bead, or let it run.

53 photos · Sept 3, 5 & 6. The band behind the beads is the real sky over Prague on Sept 5 — blue until the light goes gold, sunset at 7:39, dusk until 8:13, the last of the blue at 8:53 — the same day/dusk/night bands the Orloj paints on its own dial.

The camera was still set to India time for the whole trip, so its timestamps run 3½ hours ahead of Prague. Every time on this page has been corrected, checked against the Old Town Hall's own tower clock in Ten Past Nine (camera 00:40, clock face about 9:10). Each dot sits at its true minute; where several were taken within a minute or two of each other they stack outward across their day's ring rather than sliding round the dial, so the angle never lies.
